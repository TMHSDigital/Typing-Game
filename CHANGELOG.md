# Changelog

Each release lists the changes people using the site can see. The version is `VERSION` in `script.js`; the footer shows it.

## Unreleased

- Books: six public-domain books, typed a page at a time, continuing where you stopped.
- Lessons: six stages from the home row to numbers, each unlocked by passing the one before.
- Common Words in Spanish, French, and Portuguese. An accent typed with a dead key counts with its letter.
- Keyboard layout emulation: Colemak, Colemak-DH, Dvorak, and Workman on any physical keyboard.
- Your code: type code pasted or loaded from a file, with its line breaks and indentation.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-25)

- Daily: one 30-second passage a day, the same for everyone, shared as a line and a grid.
- Pace caret: a second cursor at a target speed, your average, or your last run.
- Replay: watch the finished run back with typos and corrections at their recorded times.
- Save image: a 1200x630 PNG of the result, in the current theme.
- Sound sets: Tones, Mechanical, Typewriter, and Soft, with a preview.
- Passage font (including OpenDyslexic), text size, line spacing, and caret style.
- Stats: misses per key on a keyboard, WPM by day, and a practice streak.
- Code Snippets: at least 25 per language. Weak Keys also practices digits and symbols.
- Settings regrouped, in two columns on wide screens. Punctuation, Numbers, language, and Skip indentation are next to the category menu.
- The live stats are one line above the passage. The result dialog leads with WPM, and its charts have a scale.
- Phones: the line being typed stays above the on-screen keyboard, and the controls hide during a run.
- A notice when progress cannot be saved. Best runs store their keystrokes in less space.
- Offline: a slow network falls back to the saved copy after 3 seconds.
- Forms for proposing quotes, snippets, and word lists.
- The footer shows the version, and returning visitors see one line about what changed.
